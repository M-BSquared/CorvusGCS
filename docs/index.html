What it does

Everything listed here is implemented.

Fast connect

On connect only what flying needs streams in. Parameters load when you open them.

Several vehicles

Every vehicle gets its own link, colour and track on one map. Take control in one press.

Message inspector

Live Messages lists every MAVLink message with its rate and live fields. Every parameter is explained.

Offline operation

Nothing loads from the internet. Download regions with their elevation ahead of time and fly without a connection.

Vehicle types

Multicopters, planes, rovers and boats on PX4 and ArduPilot. Every page shows what that firmware really has. Betaflight is in beta: its telemetry, and a read only look at the controller.

Plugins and AI clients

Four plugins ship with Corvus, written in Rust against its plugin API. An AI client can join over MCP, as far as you allow it.

Languages

English, German and French, switched with no restart. A language is one file.

Own elevation data

Load your own GeoTIFF, XYZ or ESRI ASCII grids, such as a 20 cm survey surface model. Every tree and roof stands in the 3D map.

Safety guards

Parameter writes, motor tests, flashing and ESC calibration are refused while armed. A refused write is never shown as applied.

The interface

29 screenshots from simulated flights. They play through on their own: pick one, use the arrows or press pause to stop.

Corvus GCS technical specifications
Autopilots PX4 v1.16 to v1.18, ArduPilot 4.3 to 4.6 (Copter, Plane, Rover, Boat, Sub). Betaflight 4.x is in beta: telemetry over MAVLink, for example an ExpressLRS link, and the controller read over MSP. Several vehicles at once.
Links MAVLink over serial, UDP, TCP or WebSocket. What is plugged in is found automatically, with link quality and reconnect.
RTK GPS A plugged in base station is surveyed in and RTCM 3 corrections stream to the aircraft. NTRIP works too.
Video RTSP cameras (decoded with ffmpeg) in floating windows for a second screen. A frozen picture is never passed off as live.
Maps & 3D 6 map services with 20 layers, a 3D map with terrain and OpenStreetMap buildings, and an offline tile store. Your own surface models load as the ground.
Flight logs ULog .ulg review in Flight Review. ArduPilot .bin downloads but is not parsed. Corvus's own recording reviews on both stacks.
Tools MAVLink console, SSH terminal, an MCP server for AI clients, and four plugins: Vibration Monitor, SSH Launcher, Schwalby and Map Entities.
Platforms macOS 12 or later .dmg, Linux x86_64 .AppImage, Windows x64 .zip. Nothing else to install.
From source A Rust toolchain and ./run.sh. egui on OpenGL.

Why it exists

The ground stations I used loaded every parameter first, felt slow on a field laptop and left log review to other tools. I wanted one that is ready in seconds, reviews flights itself and can be extended with plugins.

In my day-to-day work as a PhD researcher there is very little room for AI-assisted, "vibe-coded" development, because the setting simply does not allow for it. That method is nonetheless becoming a real part of how software gets built, and the only honest way to find out where it works, where it breaks, and how to direct it well is to use it seriously on something non-trivial.
